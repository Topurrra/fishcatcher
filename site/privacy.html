Privacy policy

FishCatcher works for you, not on you.

Applies to the FishCatcher browser extension.

Summary

FishCatcher does its checks on your device. There is no account, no tracking, and nothing from the pages you visit is sent anywhere. Two optional features can look something up online, and both are off until you turn them on.

What stays on your device

By default, FishCatcher runs entirely locally, using detection lists that ship inside the extension. It works with no internet connection.

  • The addresses of the pages you visit are analyzed on your device and never uploaded.
  • QR codes you check are decoded on your device.
  • Link and download checks happen in your browser.

Optional online features

These are off by default. When enabled, each does exactly one thing and nothing more.

Keep threat lists updated

Downloads a single file of known phishing sites from a fixed public location on GitHub, once a day. It only downloads. It never uploads anything about you or the pages you visit. As with any web request, the host can see your IP address and that a file was requested.

Cloud age check

Asks a public directory service (RDAP) how old a domain is. It sends only the domain name of the site being checked, and nothing else.

You can leave both off and FishCatcher still protects you with its local checks.

What is stored on your device

FishCatcher stores a small amount of data in your browser's local storage, on your device only:

  • Your settings (which features are on, your language choice).
  • Sites you mark as trusted.
  • A cache tag so updates only download when the list has changed.

This never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the extension.

Permissions

FishCatcher asks for the minimum it needs. Network permissions for the optional features are requested only at the moment you turn a feature on.

  • Current tab and pages, to read the address and page content of the tab you are on so it can check it.
  • Local storage, for your settings and trusted sites.
  • Downloads and notifications, requested only if you enable the download guard.

What we never do

  • No analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, no third-party trackers.
  • No selling or sharing of any data.
  • No account and no profile.

Because the extension is open source, you can verify all of this by reading the code.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here and in the source repository. Questions or concerns can be raised on the GitHub project.
