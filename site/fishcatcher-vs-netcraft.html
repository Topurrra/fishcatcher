Fish Catcher vs Netcraft

Updated 19 August 2026 View source
Comparison

Fish Catcher checks pages on your device and tells you in plain words why one looks risky. The Netcraft extension checks each site against Netcraft’s servers and feeds its takedown operation.

Which to pick

Pick Fish Catcher if

  • You want nothing about your browsing sent to a third party.
  • You want the reason behind every warning, in plain words.
  • You want code you can read and no account to create.

Pick Netcraft if

  • You want the widest coverage of freshly reported phishing sites.
  • You want a human team behind the feed, taking sites down.
  • You are fine with each site address being looked up in the cloud.

Where Fish Catcher is weaker

  • It is new. Netcraft has years of operating history.
  • Its blocklist is smaller, so a freshly reported site can reach Netcraft’s feed first.
  • It only warns. It will not stop a page from loading.
  • There is no takedown operation behind it. It protects you; it does not remove the site.

Common questions

Can I run Fish Catcher and Netcraft at the same time?

Yes. You may see two warnings on the same site; read whichever reason is clearer.

Does Fish Catcher send the sites I visit anywhere?

Not by default. If you turn on the optional Safe Browsing layer, that one check runs with your own Google key.
